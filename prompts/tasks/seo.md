# SEO Task

Purpose: Define the prompt contract for search-optimization planning.

- Article title: `{{TITLE}}`
- Required outcome: Recommend accurate Korean search metadata, a primary keyword,
  supporting keywords, and a concise meta description.
- Do not promise rankings, invent search-volume data, or use unsupported claims.
- Treat the Approved Article Brief as the source of truth: use its Primary Keyword
  and Supporting Long-tail Keywords first. Keep the wording natural and within
  what the article can actually answer; do not repeat keywords mechanically.
